STEP 2B: RAW VS EGO-MOTION-CORRECTED FORECASTING ABLATION
==============================================================================

Fair comparison rules:
- Same BIRDSAI TestReal core windows in raw and corrected representations.
- Raw tracks come from cleaned_track_points.csv before ego-motion compensation.
- Corrected tracks come from corrected_*.npz after ego-motion compensation.
- Both are converted from 30 Hz to 10 Hz using every third frame.
- Stationary and Constant-Velocity require no retraining, so this is a clean representation-level ablation.
- Positive improvement means corrected trajectory has lower forecasting error.
