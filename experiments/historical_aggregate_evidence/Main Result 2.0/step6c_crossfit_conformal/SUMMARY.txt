STEP 6C: TRACK-LEVEL CROSS-FITTED CONFORMAL CALIBRATION
==============================================================================

Protocol:
- All TrainReal tracks receive out-of-fold predictions.
- Three outer folds are track-disjoint.
- Outer held-out tracks are never used for training or early stopping.
- Each outer training complement has a separate inner track-disjoint validation split for early stopping.
- Three Transformer seeds (42/52/62) are trained per outer fold.
- Training weights reproduce the original exact rule: each training track contributes equal total weight.
- Primary conformal unit is TRACK, not sliding window.
- Primary track score is the maximum canonical Euclidean forecast error over all windows and future steps in that track.
- This handles within-track overlapping-window dependence more conservatively than window-level conformal calibration.
- Existing TestReal and Fresh WildBox were already inspected during Step 6A/6B; Step 6C results on them are retrospective/diagnostic and are NOT a new untouched confirmatory uncertainty test.
- A new untouched uncertainty holdout is still required before making a final confirmatory coverage claim.
