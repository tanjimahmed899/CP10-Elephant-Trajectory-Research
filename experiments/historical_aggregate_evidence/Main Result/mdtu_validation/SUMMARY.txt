MDT-U — TRAINREAL VALIDATION EXPERIMENT
==================================================================================

PROTOCOL
- Training: BIRDSAI TrainReal neural-training tracks only.
- Validation: fixed track-disjoint TrainReal validation only.
- BIRDSAI TestReal NOT loaded.
- WildBox NOT loaded.
- Same three random seeds: 42, 52, 62.
- Checkpoint selection: validation track-macro ADE.

ARCHITECTURE
- Compact temporal Transformer encoder.
- Input per observation step: [x,y,dx,dy,ddx,ddy].
- Future displacement-increment decoder.
- Future positions reconstructed by cumulative sum.
- Endpoint-aware trajectory loss.
- Increment and acceleration-consistency losses.
- Heteroscedastic Gaussian uncertainty head.
