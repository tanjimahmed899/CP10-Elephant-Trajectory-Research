UNIFIED ELEPHANT TRAJECTORY DATASET — 10 HZ
========================================================================

Common representation:
- Sampling frequency: 10 Hz
- Observation: 2 s / 20 frames
- Forecast: 2 s / 20 frames or 5 s / 50 frames
- Features used for common model: camera-corrected normalized x,y
- Both absolute and relative-to-last-observation coordinates are saved.

Experimental roles:
- BIRDSAI TrainReal CORE = training
- BIRDSAI TestReal CORE = untouched in-domain test
- WildBox RGB = untouched external cross-modal test
