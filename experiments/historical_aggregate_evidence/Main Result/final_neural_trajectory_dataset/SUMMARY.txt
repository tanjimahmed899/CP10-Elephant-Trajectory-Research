FINAL NEURAL TRAJECTORY DATASET
============================================================================

Input representation:
1. Camera-motion corrected normalized x,y
2. 10 Hz sampling
3. Last observed point translated to origin
4. Recent heading aligned to +X
5. Divided by observed history path length
6. Scale floor = TrainReal 5th percentile of history path length

Important:
- Scale floor derived from BIRDSAI TrainReal ONLY.
- Validation split is TRACK-DISJOINT.
- BIRDSAI TestReal remains untouched.
- WildBox remains untouched external test.
- Final ADE/FDE must be evaluated after inverse transform in original corrected normalized x,y.
- Training sample weights equalize total contribution of each training track.
