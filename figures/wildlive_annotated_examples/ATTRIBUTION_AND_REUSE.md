# WildLive annotated visual examples — third-party attribution and reuse

**Dataset:** *WildLive*, Nguyen Ngoc et al. (2026), University of Bristol Research Data Repository. DOI: https://doi.org/10.5523/bris.ewnuoroebuae20vei0cu6zu69

**Source dataset catalogue:** https://data.bristol.ac.uk/data/dataset/ewnuoroebuae20vei0cu6zu69

**Listed licence:** UK Non-Commercial Government Licence for public sector information, version 2: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/non-commercial-government-licence/version/2/

The six files here are selected researcher-produced **annotated contact-sheet / cropped visualization derivatives** of source WildLive aerial images, containing animal bounding boxes, ID labels, frame captions and/or image comparisons. These are not original detector, ByteTrack, or new model outputs. Reuse of these source-derived figures must respect the source licence. The project's public code visibility does **not** relicense third-party imagery or grant permission for commercial uses.

The dataset catalogue lists a non-commercial licence, but the depositor's actual rights over the exact source footage, any third-party exclusions, and compatibility with planned journal/commercial reuse must be checked by the researcher before public release. If usage conditions are not met, exclude the JPEGs and retain source links and provenance records only. Do not include original videos, entire source-frame archives, precise wildlife locations, or camera/GPS metadata.

**Suggested attribution for figures:** “Representative WildLive aerial elephant annotation visualizations (derived from Nguyen Ngoc et al., *WildLive*, University of Bristol, 2026, DOI: 10.5523/bris.ewnuoroebuae20vei0cu6zu69; source licence: Non-Commercial Government Licence v2). Bounding-box and identity-label overlays are derived research visualizations.”
